Recent changes at federal funders

    Programs are compiled from official funder pages, grants.gov funding notices, national-lab and university fellowship pages, job boards (Academic Jobs Online, INSPIRE and the AAS Job Register) and Yale's public funding newsletters, and rechecked weekly. Open means a deadline is posted. Rolling means applications are accepted at any time or until the position is filled. Watch means the next call or deadline is not posted yet or could not be confirmed. Research areas follow the Yale Applied Physics research page. Yale's full limited-submission list is in InfoReady (NetID required), so an internal date marked unverified should be confirmed there. Deadlines move: check the linked page before committing.
