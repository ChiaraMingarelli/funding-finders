What changed in 2025–26

    Programs are compiled from official funder pages, grants.gov funding notices, national-lab and university fellowship pages, and Yale's public funding newsletters, and rechecked weekly. Research areas follow the Yale Astronomy research page. Yale's full limited-submission list is in InfoReady (NetID required), so an internal date marked unverified should be confirmed there. Deadlines move: check the linked page before committing.
