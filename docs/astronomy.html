What changed in 2025–26

    Programs are compiled from official funder and observatory pages, grants.gov funding notices, national-lab and university fellowship pages, job boards (the AAS Job Register, Academic Jobs Online and INSPIRE) and Yale's public funding newsletters, and rechecked weekly. Open means a deadline is posted. Rolling means applications are accepted at any time or until the position is filled. Watch means the next call or deadline is not posted yet or could not be confirmed. Research areas follow the Yale Astronomy research page. Yale's full limited-submission list is in InfoReady (NetID required), so an internal date marked unverified should be confirmed there. Deadlines move: check the linked page before committing.
