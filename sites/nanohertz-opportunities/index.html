Fellowships, summer programs, postdoc openings and faculty job ads for early-career researchers in pulsar timing, gravitational waves and astrophysics.

Maintained by Chiara Mingarelli, Department of Physics, Yale University, for NANOGrav students and postdocs. This is not an official NANOGrav page. Made with Claude Opus 5.5 by Anthropic. We are grateful to the Yale FAS Dean of Science for his support. Entries come from funder pages, Academic Jobs Online, the AAS Job Register, INSPIRE and grants.gov, and are rechecked weekly, with new postings added between rechecks. Always confirm details on the official page before applying. Open means a deadline is posted. Rolling means applications are accepted at any time or until the position is filled. Watch means the next call or deadline is not posted yet or could not be confirmed. Fit dots are a rough guide to how closely an opportunity matches pulsar-timing and gravitational-wave work.
